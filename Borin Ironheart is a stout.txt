Borin Ironheart is a stout dwarf cleric clad in scorched plate armor that clanks noisily with every step. His braided red beard reaches his belt and is singed at the edges from working long hours at the forge. He carries a massive hammer decorated with glowing runes and smells perpetually of smoke and ale. Borin is loud, gregarious, and believes that any problem can be solved with enough faith and blunt force. He is currently searching for rare metals to craft an artifact worthy of his deity.
