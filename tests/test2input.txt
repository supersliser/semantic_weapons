Jaxen Vane is a street-level mercenary operating in the neon-drenched lower sectors of a sprawling cyberpunk metropolis. He is a lean, cybernetically enhanced human who wears a heavily patched synthetic trench coat over reinforced urban tactical gear. Half of his face is obscured by a matte-black ocular visor that glows with a faint red optic sensor, and his left arm is a fully mechanical, chrome-plated prosthetic. His weapon of choice is a high-frequency thermal katana, a sleek, devastating blade that heats to a blinding white-hot temperature, allowing it to slice through heavy corporate security armor and reinforced blast doors with ease. Jaxen is fiercely cynical, highly professional, and operates on a strict personal code that dictates he only takes contracts against the corrupt megacorporations that run the gleaming city above. Originally a high-level corporate enforcer, he went rogue after a job went horribly wrong and resulted in the collateral death of his partner. He now takes odd jobs from underground resistance factions, using his insider knowledge and deadly close-quarters skills to sabotage corporate assets while trying to scrape together enough credits to leave the planet for good.
