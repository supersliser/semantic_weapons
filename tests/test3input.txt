Runa is a wasteland drifter navigating the irradiated salt flats of a post-apocalyptic Earth. She is small but fiercely muscular, dressed in piecemeal armor crafted from repurposed tires, rusted road signs, and sun-faded canvas. A pair of cracked aviator goggles rests on her forehead, holding back a tangled mane of sun-bleached hair, and her skin is perpetually coated in a fine layer of gray ash. She fights with a massive, makeshift cleaver fashioned from the sharpened leaf spring of a heavy-duty truck, its handle wrapped in frayed paracord and stained leather. The heavy blade is crude but terrifyingly effective at chopping through scrap metal and mutant carapace alike. Runa is hyper-vigilant, deeply resourceful, and views the world entirely through the lens of survival, often hoarding seemingly useless junk that she can later jury-rig into traps or tools. Having been born long after the bombs fell, she has no concept of the old world's morality, operating on a harsh but fair barter system where water and ammunition are the only true currencies. After her nomadic caravan was scattered by a brutal warlord, she now travels alone save for a loyal, mutated hound, tirelessly searching the dead wastes for rumors of a lush, uncontaminated valley.
