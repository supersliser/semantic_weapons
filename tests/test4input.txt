Seraphina von Althain is the High Champion of the Sun-Throne, operating within the opulent and treacherous courts of a high-fantasy renaissance kingdom. She is a striking and imposing figure, clad in custom-fitted, gold-chased plate armor worn over rich sapphire velvet, with her silver hair braided tightly into a crown upon her head. She wields the Sun-Bringer, a massive, two-handed flamberge with an undulating blade forged from fallen star-metal, its gilded crossguard set with flawless rubies. Despite its ceremonial beauty, the weapon is devastatingly sharp, designed to sweep through multiple assassins or sever the heads of treasonous lords in a single, elegant motion. Seraphina is profoundly disciplined, commanding every room she enters with a cold, unwavering authority, and she views the endless gossiping of the nobility with undisguised contempt. Born as the youngest daughter of a disgraced and impoverished noble house, she single-handedly reclaimed her family's honor by winning the bloody decennial royal tournament. Now serving directly as the Queen's personal bodyguard and executioner, she stands as an incorruptible pillar of law, fully prepared to bathe the marble floors of the palace in blood to protect the crown.
