Elara Vance is a wandering spellblade who wields a unique weapon known as a whip-sword, a segmented blade that can switch between a rigid longsword and a deadly, far-reaching spiked whip with the flick of her wrist. She is striking in appearance, wearing reinforced crimson silks and dark leather boots, with pale skin, vibrant amber eyes, and short-cropped black hair. Elara possesses an outwardly charming and theatrical personality, often treating combat as a performance to distract her foes, though underneath she is deeply calculating and fiercely protective of her independence. Born into a strict noble house that demanded she become a cloistered magical scholar, she stole her family's ancestral weapon and fled into the night to forge her own path. She now travels from town to town, taking on dangerous bounties and uncovering lost magical artifacts, all while avoiding the relentless bounty hunters her family has sent to bring her back.
